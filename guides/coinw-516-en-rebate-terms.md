## Comparison context

Based on the exchanges' recently published official program pages, Binance (often shortened to BN) and Bitget advertise affiliate commission of up to 50%. Bybit's standard referral program lists 20% to 30%, while its affiliate terms vary by account and client tier. OKX lets an affiliate configure the split on a link but does not publish one universal rate for every account and region. CoinW's offer in this article is described as an 88% regular rebate plus a 2% campaign subsidy, up to 90% combined. These figures are not directly equivalent: some describe affiliate commission ceilings and others describe the share returned to an invitee. Always compare the rate actually shown on the registration and fee pages.

## Risk boundary

The combined 90% figure is campaign wording; actual eligibility and settlement depend on the rules shown for the account.

Rebates, bonuses, and eligibility can change. A rebate does not remove market, leverage, liquidation, slippage, custody, or operational risk. Do not increase trading volume or position size only to pursue a promotion.

## Quick FAQ

### Does the referral code guarantee a fixed cash payment?

No. Eligibility, covered products, regions, calculation, and settlement depend on the campaign rules and the account page.

### Can a rebate offset trading losses?

No. Trading outcomes and promotional terms are separate.

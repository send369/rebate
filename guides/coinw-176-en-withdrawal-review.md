## Risk boundary

Treat any private message demanding an extra transfer to unlock a withdrawal as high risk.

Rebates, bonuses, and eligibility can change. A rebate does not remove market, leverage, liquidation, slippage, custody, or operational risk. Do not increase trading volume or position size only to pursue a promotion.
